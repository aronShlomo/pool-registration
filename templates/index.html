About Millrod Swim Academy

Welcome to Millrod Swim Academy. We are Noam and Rivka Millrod, a husband-and-wife swim instruction team dedicated to providing professional, high-quality swimming lessons. With more than 30 years of combined teaching experience, we have helped hundreds of children and adults develop confidence, improve their swimming abilities, and build lifelong water safety skills.

Our lessons are designed for every level — from beginners learning their first strokes to experienced swimmers looking to improve technique, endurance, and efficiency. Every swimmer receives personalized instruction in a supportive, encouraging environment where safety always comes first.

Our goal is simple: help every student become a confident, safe, and capable swimmer.
